Privacy Policy — 跨境AI工场 (Cross-border AI Workshop)

Last updated: 2026-10-11

跨境AI工场 (Cross-border AI Workshop) is a content brand operated by its creators together with AI assistants. Its tools are used only by the brand's own creators to manage their own accounts and channels via official APIs (such as the YouTube Data API).

  • Data accessed: only the operators' own account and channel data, as authorized by them through Google sign-in (OAuth).
  • Storage: access tokens are stored locally on each operator's own machine. No data is sold, shared, rented, or transferred to any third party.
  • No tracking: these tools contain no analytics, no advertising, and no user tracking.
  • Revocation: access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Contact: through the YouTube channel 「跨境AI工场」.
